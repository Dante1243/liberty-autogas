Significant savings

There are significant savings to be made by switching to autogas. You will save up to 50% on your present fuel expenditure. To run a typical 4 cylinder delivery van on petrol can cost approx $1000.00 per month. That can be reduced to approx $500 by switching to LPG. In addition your engine will run smoother, cleaner and will last far longer.

Lower running costs

Maintenance costs will be reduced because autogas burns cleaner with far less harmful exhaust emissions. That's a plus for the environment as well.

LPG is an abundant fuel source the world over, and it can be produced from crude oil stock as well as natural gas, a resource of which Australia has vast deposits. Because of these various sources, it is a lot less likely to be subject to shortages.

Figures are a general guide. Call us and we'll talk through what your vehicle could save.
