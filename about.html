About Liberty Autogas Conversions

A West Australian family owned & operated business, operating in the Rockingham area since 2006.

Family owned, LPG focused

Liberty Autogas Conversions is a West Australian family owned & operated business, which has been operating in the Rockingham area since 2006.

Dennis Kenworthy, known as ‘The Gas Guru’, is the owner, with personal experience in the automotive gas industry since 1985. Over this time Dennis has perfected his own Diesel/LPG conversion system that is used on all our diesel conversions.

Diesel & petrol conversions

With Dennis's system we can convert your 4WD, car, ute or stationary engine. The LPG works in harmony with your diesel, is environmentally friendly and, more importantly, saves you money! The conversion in no way affects the normal operation of the vehicle.

We also do Petrol/LPG conversions along with petrol and diesel servicing, using only best quality parts.
