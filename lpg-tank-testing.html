LPG Tank Testing

What you need to know, and why it pays to use a licensed specialist who does the work in-house.

Every 10 years, by law

To comply with Australian regulations, LPG tank testing must be done every 10 years. By law, all LPG tanks must be inspected, tested and certified by a licensed LPG operator with specialised LPG tank testing equipment to comply with Australian standards.

Testing done in-house

Liberty Auto Gas are the leading LPG tank testing specialists in Perth, with over 40 years experience in the automotive LPG industry. We are a fully licensed LP Gas testing facility and all work is done in-house. That's right, our workshop is fully equipped with state-of-the-art LPG tank testing equipment that many other workshops simply don't have.

Many other workshops, despite being able to work on LPG powered vehicles, do not have the appropriate LPG licence or tank testing equipment, so they bring the tanks to us for testing. So ask the question: do you do the work yourself or outsource it to someone else?

More than a legal requirement

Having your LPG tank tested isn't just about meeting the legal requirements, it's also about making sure your LPG fuel delivery system is operating correctly. A defective installation will lead to poor fuel economy, which will cost you money.
